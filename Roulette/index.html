Rules & Payouts

Inside Bets

  • Straight (1 number) pays 35:1
  • Split (2 numbers) pays 17:1
  • Street (3 numbers) pays 11:1
  • Corner (4 numbers) pays 8:1
  • Line (6 numbers) pays 5:1

Outside Bets

  • Dozens & Columns pay 2:1
  • Red/Black, Even/Odd, 1-18/19-36 pay 1:1
  • Zero is green and loses for outside bets

House Tips

  • You can place multiple bets per round.
  • Use the bet slip to remove bets before spinning.
  • Deposit adds in-game money only.
